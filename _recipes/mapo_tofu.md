ingredients:
- zh: "花椒"
  en: "Sichuan Peppercorns"
- zh: "豆腐"
  en: "Tofu"
- zh: "牛肉末"
  en: "Ground Beef"
- zh: "姜"
  en: "Ginger"
- zh: "蒜"
  en: "Garlic"
- zh: "豆瓣酱"
  en: "Doubanjiang (Fermented Bean Paste)"
- zh: "豆豉"
  en: "Fermented Black Beans"
- zh: "辣椒面"
  en: "Chili Powder"
- zh: "青蒜"
  en: "Green Garlic/Leek Sprouts"

directions:
- zh: "铁锅烧热，下入大量花椒，余温焙香"
  en: "Heat a wok, add a generous amount of Sichuan peppercorns and toast them using residual heat."
- zh: "花椒放入保鲜膜，碾碎过筛"
  en: "Place peppercorns in plastic wrap, crush and sieve."
- zh: "豆腐切成大拇指宽，加入老抽和盐焯水，水开后1min关火，泡着"
  en: "Cut tofu into thumb-sized pieces, blanch in water with dark soy sauce and salt. Turn off heat after 1 minute of boiling, let soak."
- zh: "牛肉末炒干，加入姜末，炒至焦脆，盛出。注意火太大肉末会结块"
  en: "Stir-fry ground beef until dry, add minced ginger, stir-fry until crispy, then remove. Be careful, high heat can cause the meat to clump."
- zh: "起锅烧油，蒜末炒香，加入剁碎的豆瓣酱和豆豉炒香"
  en: "Heat oil in the wok, sauté minced garlic until fragrant, add chopped doubanjiang and fermented black beans, stir-fry until fragrant."
- zh: "加入一勺半的辣椒面炒香，加入一勺半生抽"
  en: "Add 1.5 spoons of chili powder and stir-fry until fragrant, then add 1.5 spoons of light soy sauce."
- zh: "加入一碗半清水，一茶勺鸡精，一勺白糖"
  en: "Add 1.5 bowls of water, 1 tsp chicken bouillon, and 1 tbsp sugar."
- zh: "水开放入豆腐，青蒜蒜白"
  en: "Once water boils, add tofu and the white parts of green garlic/leek sprouts."
- zh: "水收至剩下1/3时，勾芡一次，随后加入牛肉末"
  en: "When the sauce reduces to 1/3, thicken with cornstarch. Then add the ground beef."
- zh: "勾芡一次，加入青蒜叶，再次勾芡，完成"
  en: "Thicken with cornstarch again, add green garlic/leek leaves, thicken one last time. Serve."
