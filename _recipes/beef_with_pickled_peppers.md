  en: "Marinate the beef, pat dry and set aside."

  en: "Squeeze out liquid from two spoons of pickled chilies, chop, and mix with minced garlic sauce. Set aside."

  en: "Dice ginger, garlic, and bird's eye chilies. Cut celery into sections."

  en: "Mix 1 tbsp light soy sauce, 1 tbsp dark soy sauce, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 1.5 tbsp Sichuan peppercorn oil, starch, chicken bouillon, and 4 tbsp water."
- zh: "牛肉炒至七分熟，盛出"
  en: "Stir-fry beef until 70% cooked, then remove from pan."
- zh: "先加泡椒、姜和蒜蓉酱煸香"
  en: "First, sauté pickled chilies, ginger, and minced garlic sauce until fragrant."
- zh: "再加蒜末小米辣"
  en: "Then add minced garlic and bird's eye chilies."
- zh: "加入芹菜炒1min至断生"
  en: "Add celery and stir-fry for 1 minute until crisp-tender."
- zh: "牛肉回锅，酱汁沿着锅煸淋入，完成"
  en: "Return beef to the wok, pour in the sauce along the edge of the pan, and serve."
