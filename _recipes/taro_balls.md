  zh: "甜点"

ingredients:
- zh: "芋头"
  en: "Taro"
- zh: "红薯"
  en: "Sweet Potato"
- zh: "木薯淀粉"
  en: "Tapioca Starch"
- zh: "白砂糖"
  en: "Granulated Sugar"

directions:
- zh: "芋头与红薯切片蒸熟，压成泥"
  en: "Slice and steam taro and sweet potato until cooked, then mash into a paste."
- zh: "每200g芋头加入120g木薯淀粉与25g白砂糖，少量开水"
  en: "For every 200g of taro, add 120g tapioca starch, 25g granulated sugar, and a small amount of boiling water."
- zh: "每200g红薯加入100g木薯淀粉与20g白砂糖"
  en: "For every 200g of sweet potato, add 100g tapioca starch and 20g granulated sugar."
- zh: "揉面，后各取1/3压扁放入开水中煮2min"
  en: "Knead the dough. Then take 1/3 of each, flatten, and boil in water for 2 minutes."
- zh: "取出擦干，与剩下2/3混合"
  en: "Remove, pat dry, and mix with the remaining 2/3 of the dough."
- zh: "揉长，切块，注意别切太大，煮开的时候会膨胀"
  en: "Roll into long ropes, then cut into pieces. Be careful not to cut too large, as they will expand when boiled."
